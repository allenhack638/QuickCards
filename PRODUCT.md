# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

Primary user: the phone's owner, managing payment cards for themselves and for other people they look after (several card holders, for example family members) on a personal Android phone. The recurring situation is needing a specific card's number, expiry, CVV, or note quickly — during an online checkout or while standing at a payment terminal, often one-handed — and getting back out. The job is retrieval and action, not account management or financial tracking.

Distribution is a signed APK published on GitHub Releases, not through an app store.

## Product Purpose

QuickCards is a local-first vault for the user's payment cards. Card details that would otherwise live in a wallet, photos, or a notes app are stored encrypted, opened instantly, and copied in a few taps. Success means the fastest possible path from opening the app to the exact value the user needs, with nothing leaving the device.

## Positioning

Fast daily card access on a device-local vault for one or several card holders. The differentiator is the combination: retrieval speed as the primary design goal (copy straight from the card list), while every sensitive value stays encrypted at rest, masked on screen, and gated behind one biometric or device-credential unlock that opens a short, visible instant-copy window. A cloud wallet cannot truthfully claim the offline guarantee; a plain notes app cannot claim authenticated, time-boxed reveals.

## Operating Context

- Android phone, `minSdk 26`; both orientations are permitted.
- Used in short, high-frequency sessions: open, find a card, copy a number or expiry, leave.
- The app opens to an app lock screen (configurable, on by default) and asks for the fingerprint or screen lock straight away; the Unlock button re-opens the prompt after a cancel, and the lock screen shows the app version. The app locks again after a configurable period without use (10 seconds to Never, default 1 minute) and when the user leaves the app (configurable, on). Opening the system file picker for an import or export does not count as leaving if the user is back within 5 minutes; sharing a card gets 1 minute. Back later than that, the app locks as if the user had left, and the inactivity lock always counts the time away. The app appears in Recents like any other app (its preview is blanked by FLAG_SECURE in release builds).
- Reminders (local notifications, private on the lock screen, bank and last four only): 30 days before the end of a card's expiry month (it fires once, keeps its fire date, and tapping it opens that card's details), and 3 days before a card's optional payment due day (set per card in the form, 1-31, clamped to short months). An expiry reminder fires once; a payment reminder repeats once a day inside its 3-day window until the user taps its "Paid" action, which silences it for that due date without opening the app. In the app, the card shows a "Due in N days" strip with "Mark paid" for the same window; Mark paid there and Paid on the notification are the same action, with Undo in the app. There is no popup on opening. Both kinds have a Settings switch. A daily check runs at about 09:00 and on every app start.
- Autofill: QuickCards can be Android's autofill service. In another app's checkout form the number field offers "Search all N cards…" first, then up to five cards (pinned, then most recently copied or filled, then list order; archived excluded) as "HDFC Bank ••2211" with the holder below it and the network's mark. Typing the first digits or the last four in the number field narrows that list (Android 9+; only the first six and last four digits are used for matching). "Search all N cards…" asks for the fingerprint or PIN, then opens a QuickCards list of every card with the same search as Home (digits anywhere in the number, bank, holder, network, tag, note); picking one fills the form, and Back, Cancel or leaving fills nothing. For a suggestion, tapping one opens the fingerprint/PIN prompt, and only then are number, expiry, CVV (if asked) and name filled directly into the form, never via the clipboard. A cancelled prompt fills nothing. Nothing typed in other apps is ever saved. Until Android has chosen QuickCards as its autofill service, Settings › Copying › Autofill shows "Not set up" with a "Finish setup" action that opens Android's autofill settings, and no switch; once chosen, the row is a switch that turns the feature off entirely. "Add cards by tapping" (NFC phones only) turns the tap-to-add reader off.
- Copied values stay on the clipboard for the chosen clear time even after leaving the app. On Android 13+ the clip is marked sensitive, so the system's clipboard preview does not render it; a copy whose timer was lost to process death is cleared once its window gains focus. Clear or a new copy removes it earlier.
- One successful BiometricPrompt opens an instant-copy window (30 s, 1 min default, 2 min); inside it copy, reveal, edit and delete need no further prompt; export, import, erase and switching a protection off always ask again. The window and the app's inactivity lock both count idle time only: any touch, scroll or typing restarts them, so the app never locks or re-prompts while it is being used. There is no lock-state chip or countdown on the Cards screen; the times are configurable in Settings › App lock (Auto-lock, Lock when you leave, Instant copy, shown as "No fingerprint for 1 minute") and Lock now remains in Settings.
- Card numbers are masked to the last four digits on every screen except Edit (Settings › Cards › Hide card numbers): compact surfaces (card tiles, rows, sheet headers, dialogs, notifications, autofill) show "••0366"; the Card details card and rows show "••••0366". On Home the card's Copy pill copies the full card number, and nothing else. Tapping a card opens Card details, where every value (number, expiry, CVV, holder, note) has its own Copy, and holding the number or CVV row reveals it for five seconds with a draining line. Holding a card on Home opens its card actions sheet.
- Card details can be scanned with the keyboard's own scan tool where the keyboard offers it: pasted or scanned text fills the number and expiry, never the CVV, and no camera permission is used.
- A card past the last day of its expiry month renders as a graphite card with an "Expired" status chip; it stays fully usable (copy, reveal in Card details, edit, pin, archive, delete). A card expiring within the chosen 1, 2 or 3 months (default 1) shows an "Expires soon" status chip and is otherwise unchanged. Archived cards live in Manage › Archived, and a card's note can show on its tile. Settings › Cards holds those three choices.
- CVV is always masked by default and re-masks five seconds after a reveal. In Add and Edit the CVV field has a "Show CVV" eye that shows it for five seconds and re-masks on leaving the field.
- After a card number is copied, the Expiry and CVV notification shows that card's expiry and CVV for 30 seconds (Notification time is configurable; hidden on the lock screen, kept in the shade while the user switches apps) with "Copy expiry" and "Copy CVV" actions, so each is one tap from the shade; the actions copy directly without a second unlock, because the notification only appears after an unlocked copy, and they are never shown on the lock screen. It is silent and not a heads-up. It is on by default for new installs; existing installs keep their setting, and turning it on later asks to unlock. Expiry and CVV are also copied from Card details. On Android 13 and later the notification permission is asked the first time a card number is copied (not at install); if it is declined, copying still works and Card details remains the way to copy expiry and CVV, and reminders stay off until the permission is granted.
- Data moves between installs only through the user-initiated encrypted export/import in Settings, protected by a backup password.
- No network is required or used.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed functionality:

- Home is a list of card tiles: bank, type and holder, the masked number, expiry, the network mark, one status chip and a Copy pill. The Copy pill copies the card number only; tapping a card opens Card details (number, expiry, CVV, holder and note, each with its own Copy; hold the number or CVV to reveal it); holding a card opens the card actions sheet. A copy shows "Copied" in place and is announced to TalkBack; single-value copies never show a snackbar. The most recently copied card shows "Last used" when it has no other status, and the list never reorders. Holder chips (All, then each holder) filter the list.
- A card shows at most one status chip, text only, by priority Archived > Expired > Check > Expires soon > Last used; the tile and Card details always show the same status.
- First run: three tips shown once ("Tap to copy. Hold for more." · "Encrypted on this phone" · "Add your first card") with Skip and Next, then the empty vault: a faded sample card, "Add your first card", "Encrypted. Stays on this phone.", a full-width Add card button and equal Import and Tap a card (NFC phones only) buttons. Search is hidden on the empty vault; + stays.
- Launcher shortcuts: Add card and Search (static), Tap to add (dynamic, NFC phones only); each passes the lock first.
- Without a device screen lock, Home shows the banner "Set a screen lock to copy" with a Settings action that opens the screen lock settings.
- Launcher, themed, notification, splash and lock-screen marks use the Stub Lock logo.
- The ten built-in networks (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, RuPay, Diners Club, Discover, JCB, Maestro, UnionPay, Mir) show their official mark with no plate, on the card, in Manage, in the card form and in autofill: full colour on light surfaces, white on card art and in the dark theme (Mastercard and Maestro always full colour). Custom networks keep the text label.
- Card create and edit with card holder, card number, expiry, CVV, bank, card type (required since 2.6; a card saved earlier without one asks for it when edited), tags, card colour and note. Empty fields show placeholders (Choose holder, 0000 0000 0000 0000, MM/YY, 123, Choose bank, Credit or debit, and Bank colour for the colour); format rules show only as errors ("Month: 1 to 12", "Enter 3 digits"). A disabled Save names the first missing or wrong item in form order ("Choose a holder", "Add the number", "Check the number" …); Edit enables Save once something changes. The card holder is the only name: the card shows the holder's name (the old "name on card" and "card name" fields are gone from the form; their stored values are kept but unused). The network always follows the number's first digits (longest prefix match) and shows only as its mark in the number field and on the card; there is no network field or manual override. A number that matches no network shows no mark and is checked with the generic rule (12-19 digits, Luhn).
- Card colour: sixteen palette colours (graphite included) or a custom colour that keeps white text at 7:1 or more ("Too light. Pick a darker shade." disables "Use colour" otherwise), chosen in the Colour bottom sheet. Every bank has a default colour; picking a bank in Add card applies it unless this card's colour was chosen by hand, and that override stays with the card ("Bank colour" resets it). Each colour has one fixed pattern, never random (a custom colour's pattern comes from its hex), so changing a card's colour changes its pattern.
- Card details can be scanned with the keyboard's own scan tool where the keyboard offers it (Card number and Expiry use the text keyboard, with suggestions off, so the scan tool is available; CVV keeps the masked number pad); pasted or scanned text is cleaned and fills number and expiry (never CVV). No camera permission. A single input event that inserts two or more characters is parsed through the normal number rules (grouping, network detection, limits, validation); an expiry fills an empty Expiry field. Typing one character at a time is unchanged, junk text keeps the field's digit filter, and a fill shows "Number pasted" or "Number and expiry pasted".
- Legacy card data is repaired non-destructively, once on upgrade and on every import. Numbers keep digits only; expiry accepts MM/YY, M/YY, MM//YY, MM-YY, MM.YY, MM YY, MMYY, MM/YYYY, MMYYYY, YYYY-MM and YY/MM (only when the first part is over 12 and the second is a month); CVV keeps digits only at the network's length. Anything unparseable keeps its value and is flagged instead of failing. The first repair shows one dismissible one-line Cards banner ("N fixed · M to check") with Review and a close button (TalkBack "Dismiss"); Review turns on a "Needs check (M)" filter. Flagged cards show a "Check" status chip (TalkBack "Check details") and the field's error in Edit. Before values are kept encrypted for 30 days; a field whose value changed shows "Changed from …" with Undo (a change of separators only shows nothing). Past expiry dates are valid (expired cards are real data).
- Expired and expires-soon cards: a card is expired after the last day of its expiry month (local date) and renders as a graphite card (the graphite colour with its lines pattern) with the "Expired" status chip; text keeps full contrast and copy, reveal in Card details, edit, pin, archive and delete all keep working. A card expiring within 1, 2 or 3 months (default 1) shows an "Expires soon" status chip only and does not change order. Settings › Cards › Expired cards chooses Keep in place, At the end (default) or Hide from the list; pinned expired cards stay pinned, and hidden cards still appear in search and when the Expired filter is on. The filter sheet has a Status group (Expired, Expires soon) with counts.
- Archive: the card actions sheet offers Archive above Delete. The dialog "Archive card?" says "Moves to Manage › Archived." and takes an optional 40-character reason (placeholder "Card closed"). Archiving unpins the card, shows "Card archived" with Undo, and moves it to Manage › Archived: a screen with the same tiles, a Search field (last 4, bank, holder, network, type, tags, note, reason), the empty state "No archived cards" with the hint "Hold a card to archive it", the no-results state, and Restore, Copy details, Edit and Delete in each card's actions sheet. Restore shows "Card restored" with Undo. Archived cards also appear in the main search, labelled "Archived · reason". Archive state and reason travel in backups.
- Notes on cards: Settings › Cards › Notes on cards (default on) shows the note under the type and holder line of each tile, at most two lines with an ellipsis and nothing else (no More/Less); TalkBack reads the whole note and the full note is on Card details. Holding the note copies it ("Note copied" with Clear). Notes stay searchable, appear in the card actions sheet header, and are shown in Archived.
- Card formats: 12 to 19 digit numbers with per-network grouping and masking (Visa 13/16/19, Mastercard 16, American Express 15 with 4-digit CVV, RuPay 16, Diners Club 14-19, Discover, JCB, Maestro, UnionPay without Luhn, Mir), Luhn check unless the network disables it. The card number and expiry use the number pad. Each network has "Cards may have no CVV" (on for built-in Maestro, since many have none printed); a card on such a network may be saved without a CVV and shows "CVV None" and has nothing to copy, share or fill.
- Managed lists in a Manage tab: card holders, banks, card networks (rules editable, custom networks allowed), card types and tags. Changing a built-in network away from its standard rules asks first ("Change Visa rules?", listing the standard rules); while they differ the editor shows a "Custom rules" banner with Reset (TalkBack "Reset Visa to standard rules"), and the card form shows "Visa: custom number rules" with Reset when a changed rule is what blocks a card. Saved cards that stop fitting a changed rule show the Check chip; their values are never rewritten. Names are unique per list; renaming an item that cards use asks for confirmation ("Update 3 cards?") and lists those cards; deleting an item in use is blocked ("Can’t delete Aarav Mehta", with Show cards) until its cards are changed or deleted. The bank editor has Name and Colour: a new bank gets the first palette colour, in a fixed order, that no bank uses yet ("Not used by another bank"), or the least-used colour when all are taken. Manage lists: Holders, Banks, Networks, Types, Tags and Archived, each with its count.
- Card numbers are unique across all holders, with no exceptions. Duplicates are rejected on add and edit (Keystore-backed HMAC hash); a backup file that holds the same number twice imports only the first copy and the review shows "N skipped"; and on upgrade any duplicates left by older versions are removed automatically, keeping the copy edited most recently and carrying over the others' tags and a missing note.
- Search over digits anywhere in the number (spaces ignored), bank, holder, network, type, note and tags; results show only the last 4. Search opens on Recent (the last 3 copied cards); no match shows "No matches" and "Try a bank, holder, tag or digits" with Clear search. Filters (bottom sheet) by holder, bank, network, type, tag and status (Expired, Expires soon); holder chips on the home screen.
- Settings and card-menu behaviour: Theme is a normal Settings row opening a bottom sheet of radio rows (System default, Light, Dark); every single choice (Theme, Expired cards, Expires soon, Clear clipboard, Notification time, Auto-lock, Instant copy) is a bottom sheet of radio rows that applies on tap and closes, never a dialog. The card actions sheet (hold a card on Home, or ⋮ on Card details) has a text-only header (bank and last 4, then type and holder) and offers Copy details ("Details copied" with Clear), Share (a fresh unlock every time, then the dialog "Share card", "Sends bank, number, expiry and name as text.", with Include CVV off by default, then the system share sheet), Edit, Pin/Unpin, Archive and Delete ("Delete HDFC Bank ••0366?", "You can undo this for a few seconds.", then "Card deleted" with Undo). Settings rows that open a sheet or page end in a chevron; pinned cards stay first in pin order, carry a pin mark and are limited to 10; at the limit Pin is disabled in the actions sheet with the line "Unpin a card first" (no dialog, nothing is unpinned automatically); pins are kept in backups. One filled brand-red button per screen; Save is disabled until an edit changes something (Edit) or every required field is valid (Add); every input enforces its limits while typing; Holder, Bank and Type all use one bottom-sheet picker that always shows Search and ends with "New holder", "New bank" or "New type".
- Settings › About shows the version (the internal versionCode is not shown) and the bundled privacy policy; the offline promise lives in the privacy policy and the store listing, not in a row. On copies installed from Google Play, a new Play version is offered once through Play's own update sheet after unlock (flexible update, never repeated for the same version); a downloaded update shows "Update ready" with Restart, and the Version row reads "Update available" / "Downloading update" / "Restart to update" until it is installed. The Play Store app does the check and download; QuickCards adds no permission. The GitHub APK never shows any of this.
- Settings: Theme at the top (no group label); Cards: Hide card numbers, Notes on cards, Tags on cards, Add cards by tapping (NFC phones), Expired cards, Expires soon; Copying: Clear clipboard, Expiry and CVV notification ("Hidden on lock screen"; on by default for new installs; Notification time shows only while it is on), Autofill; Security: App lock (a sub-page with the master switch, Auto-lock, Lock when you leave and Instant copy), Lock now; Reminders: Expiry reminders, Payment reminders; Backup: Export backup, Import cards; About: Version, Privacy policy; Erase all data alone at the end. An ⓘ in the Settings bar opens a Help sheet (Instant copy, Autofill setup, Spreadsheet columns, Check), each row opening its setting or screen; there is no other help text.
- AES-256-GCM field encryption at rest for card number, expiry and CVV, and for holder, note and archive-reason metadata and the bank name stored on each card, with keys in the Android Keystore. Bank, card type, network, tag and card-variant labels are plain labels, not card data.
- AES-256-GCM export protected by a backup password (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 600k iterations). Backup format 2 contains all cards (including archive state and reason and the needs-check flags), all managed lists and, optionally (on by default), all settings including the Cards choices. Import runs the legacy normaliser, shows a review ("Formats fixed: N · Need a check: M", new/changed/same cards, new list items, settings), then updates saved cards with the backup's details (default) or keeps them, and restores settings when ticked. Removed settings keys in older files are ignored silently. Format 1 backups from earlier versions stay importable. Export stays disabled until the password has at least 8 characters and both entries match ("Must match", then "Passwords match"). Erase all data asks the user to tick "This can’t be undone" (unticked by default; Erase stays disabled until it is ticked) and then to unlock.
- Import cards from a spreadsheet (2.6): Settings › Backup › "Import cards" offers "QuickCards backup" or "Spreadsheet"; all three spreadsheet steps are titled "Spreadsheet". The sheet step offers three downloads (the Excel sheet generated on the phone with dropdowns for every list, the public Google Sheet via surety.money/quickcards/sheet, or a simple CSV plus an allowed-values CSV), then Choose file opens the file picker (.xlsx or .csv, judged by content). Every row is checked with the card form's rules plus the sheet rules (every column but due day and note required; digits-only number; the picked network must be the number's network; list-only month, year, network, bank and type; CVV at the network's length; repeats and cards already on the phone). Both "Import cards" choices and the three ways to get the sheet are standard two-line list rows (icon, title, supporting text, chevron) that run edge to edge of the dialog. The check screen shows the file row ("N rows checked", Change picks another file), a framed count list (Ready, To fix, Already saved, New holders), then "What to fix" with each failing row's number beside one line per problem (the first 25 rows; the rest are counted), "Save fix list" (a CSV of only those rows with a Problem column) and a pinned "Import N cards"; a sheet whose cards are all already saved says so. A refused file is reported in a dialog that offers "Choose another file" (and "Save blank sheet", the bundled template, for missing or unknown columns); step 1 links to the Help row "Spreadsheet columns". The done step shows "N cards added", the warning "This file has card numbers. Delete it." and "Delete file". Nothing is saved before Import; cards already on the phone are skipped. The sheet's headings and lists, the bank catalog, the networks and the card types are one definition (`ImportSchema`, `BankCatalog`), guarded by tests against the published sheet.
- Daylight light and dark themes (Theme: System default, Light, Dark; there is no black theme); ISO ID-1 card proportions (85.60 × 53.98 mm); no bank logos.
- Adaptive layout by window size class: bottom bar below 600 dp, navigation rail from 600 dp (never both); a 2-lane staggered tile grid at medium width and in phone landscape; list-detail (cards pane | Card details) on foldables and expanded windows; two-pane Settings on expanded windows; Add card is a 560 dp dialog on expanded windows. A separating hinge always splits the panes and no content crosses it.
- Three-tab navigation (Cards, Manage, Settings). Overlay rule: bottom sheets for choosing or acting (card actions, holder/bank/type/colour pickers, filters, tags, import choice, single choices); dialogs only to confirm or acknowledge (delete, erase, discard changes, NFC off, errors that need a decision); one app-level snackbar host for Undo, Clear and errors; an inline banner for a fixable screen state (no screen lock, repair, custom network rules). Sheets hug their content.
- `FLAG_SECURE` on every activity; screenshots and screen recording are blocked.
- R8-minified signed release APK and AAB for Google Play.
- Jetpack Compose with Material Design 3; package `com.quickcards.app`.

Durable constraints:

- Offline-only and camera-free: no network permission, no camera permission, no sync, no telemetry. The only Google library is Play in-app updates, which talks to the Play Store app on the phone and adds no permission. The merged debug and release manifests are verified to contain no INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE or CAMERA permission.
- Encryption is mandatory. Biometrics are mandatory for sensitive actions; opening the app is gated by the app lock when it is on (default).
- Existing data must survive changes: Room migrations must be non-destructive, and older card exports must remain importable.
- `minSdk 26` must not be raised.
- Screenshot/screen-recording prevention is mandatory and implemented with `FLAG_SECURE` on every activity.

Open decisions:

- Distribution artifact: a signed APK on GitHub Releases and Google Play (closed testing since 2.4.0), signed with the same key. Signing secrets live only in CI repository secrets and a git-ignored local file (see `keystore/README.md`).
- Bank (issuer) logos are not licensed or bundled; cards never show bank logos.

## Product Principles

1. Speed to the needed value wins: minimize open → find card → copy.
2. Nothing leaves the device; offline is a guarantee, not a degraded mode.
3. Sensitive values are masked by default; reveals are deliberate, authenticated, and time-boxed.
4. Existing card data and export compatibility are inviolable across changes.
5. Follow native Android and Material 3 expectations; express brand through theme and detail, not custom controls.
