Obsidian.
An instrument for your cards.

QuickCards is opened for ten seconds at a time: at a till, one-handed, under shop lighting or in a dark room. Obsidian treats it as a precision instrument. The chrome is near-black graphite built from luminance steps and one hairline weight. Numbers are set in Geist Mono like measured values. The only colour on screen is the card: a matte anodised slab in its band colour, lit along the top edge, with a machined groove and two notches where the stub's perforation used to be. Search sits at the bottom of the screen, under the thumb, and lights up the digits you typed inside the masked number. Nothing moves unless it answers a touch, and then it moves fast.

01

The card is the only colour.Chrome stays graphite. All sixteen bands are normalised to one lightness (OKLCH 0.42), so no card shouts and white text clears 8:1 on every one.

02

Numbers are measurements.Geist Mono, tabular, grouped like the plastic. Masked groups dim to 56 % white; the last four stay at full contrast and are the heaviest type on the screen.

03

The thumb owns retrieval.Search and filter dock above the navigation bar. Every copy target is a full-height cell of 56 dp or more. After unlock, one tap copies.

What changes from today's QuickCards

  • The card becomes the colour object. The 48 dp band over a grey body becomes a full-colour ID-1 slab. Tags, status, note and the bill strip move to a tinted sleeve that the slab sits in, so copy targets never move.
  • Search moves under the thumb. The app-bar search icon becomes a command bar docked above the navigation bar. Typed digits light up inside the masked number.
  • Seven type roles instead of eleven sizes. Roboto Flex at 9–24 sp becomes Geist and Geist Mono with real jumps. The card number outweighs every label.
  • Edges you can see. Fields, chips, checkboxes and switch tracks get outlines of at least 3:1. Selected chips invert. Bands are retuned to equal lightness.
  • One TalkBack stop per card. Each pass is one node with copy, show and menu custom actions. Headings, pane titles, focus into dialogs and back, and 200 % text that reflows the stub instead of truncating it.

Design system

Every value below comes from one token object in this file. The swatches, the contrast table in section 2b and the JSON in the appendix are all generated from it, so they cannot drift apart.

Colour

A cool graphite ladder with six steps that are visibly apart, not five steps of 2 %. Separation comes from luminance and a hairline at 7–11 % white. Outlines on anything you can operate are at least 3:1. Brand red is only ever a fill under white text, at most once per screen.

Card bands, retuned

Each band keeps its hue, loses 20 % chroma, and is set to OKLCH lightness 0.42. That puts white text at 8.2–9.2:1 on the flat band and above 7:1 at the lit top edge, and the label tint (76 % white) above 5:1. The small square in each corner is the current shipping colour.

Network plates

The ten built-in marks are drawn as simple typographic plates on white, 36 × 22 dp on the card and 56 × 28 dp in Manage. The shipping app uses the licensed artwork on the same plate. Custom networks are a text label in a 1 dp white outline.

Typography

Two families drawn together. Geist is the voice of the chrome, and 500 is its workhorse weight. Geist Mono is used only for values you read or copy: card numbers, expiry, CVV, counts and the version. Mono is never used for decoration. Sizes scale with the system font setting along Android 14's non-linear curve, so large roles grow less than small ones.

Shape and spacing

Corners are tighter than stock Material 3 Expressive and looser than Linear. Small parts use 6–10 dp, controls 12 dp, the card 16 dp, and dialogs and sheets 20–22 dp. Pills are reserved for the navigation indicator and avatars, which are the only round things. Spacing is a 4 dp grid: 8 dp inside a group, 16 dp gutters, 24–32 dp between groups.

Elevation

On near-black a shadow alone is invisible, so dark elevation is a step in tone plus a ring of 7–10 % white. A soft, offset shadow is added only for things that float: the pass, sheets, dialogs and the snackbar. The light theme lifts dialogs with shadow rather than tone, because a darker dialog over a light screen reads as a hole.

Motion

Chrome motion is standard Material, and fast. The one authored moment is the copy confirmation: the copied value lifts 4 dp and settles in 180 ms on an exponential ease-out, while a 2 dp line sweeps under the exact field you touched. With Remove animations on, the lift is skipped and the line simply appears; nothing else is lost.

Iconography

Material Symbols Rounded at weight 400, optical size 24, grade −25 on dark to stop glyphs blooming. Outline by default; filled only to mean "selected" or "on" (selected tab, pinned card). One family, no emoji, no Unicode stand-ins.

Card rendering recipe

The slab is built from six layers, all cheap enough for API 26 in a lazy list. It uses no RuntimeShader and no RenderEffect: two gradients, a cached noise tile, two inset strokes, the groove and the type. Shapes come from one Compose GenericShape with the two notches subtracted at the groove's measured position.

Components

Every control maps to a Material 3 composable, themed through the colour, type and shape tokens. Each board is rendered twice, dark and light, with the states forced so they can be compared side by side. Hover, press and keyboard focus also work live in this page.

Usability & accessibility

This section shows the work rather than listing it. The contrast table is computed live from the tokens. The touch targets and the TalkBack order are measured from the real frames on this page. The 200 % frames use the same components as everything else.
