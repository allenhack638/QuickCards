Obsidian Checkout.
Built for paying online.

Pay with autofill: tap the card field in the shop, pick a card, touch the sensor. When autofill can’t help, a card opens in place into a checkout panel, three copies for one fingerprint. The number still copies in one tap from a closed card.

Autofill is the checkout.2 taps, 1 fingerprint, 0 app switches, against 15, 3 and 6 today. The app already has it; nobody finds it. This direction offers it at the moment you pay: after your second number copy, once.
The panel is the fallback, and it is calm.A closed card is a ticket: bank above the perforation, number below. Open it and the stub grows into expiry and CVV, whole rows as targets, one glyph on one right edge.
One fingerprint, three copies.Inside the instant-copy window every row copies at once. Outside it, the first tap asks once and the next two are instant.
Two tabs.Cards and Settings. Manage becomes a Lists group inside Settings. Proposed — needs owner OK

Try the checkout card

The fallback, live. Tap the bank row or the chevron to open and close it; tap any row to copy it. Press and hold the number or CVV to show it.

Design system

One token object themes the page, the contrast table and the JSON in the appendix. Obsidian's ladder, type and bands are kept. What is new is the material for feedback: a raised graphite slab with a lit top edge for the toast, the search field and the ready panel.

Colour

Six graphite steps, one hairline weight, outlines at 3:1 or more on anything you operate. Brand red is a fill under white text, once per screen at most. The toast, the search field and the ready panel use s3 with a 1 dp ring and, in dark, a lit top edge. The system clipboard overlay is drawn in its own colours because it is not ours.

Card bands

Each band keeps its hue at OKLCH lightness 0.42, so white text clears 8:1 on every one. The small square is the current shipping colour.

Network plates

Typographic stand-ins on white, 36 × 22 dp on the card. The app ships the licensed artwork on the same plate.

Typography

Geist for the chrome, Geist Mono only for values you read or copy. In the open card, expiry and CVV are set at the card-number size, 22 sp, so the three checkout targets read as equals.

Shape and spacing

Small parts 6–10 dp, controls 12 dp, toasts 16 dp, the card 18 dp, sheets 22–28 dp, the search field 26 dp. Full pills only for the nav indicator and avatars. 4 dp grid.

Spacing scale (dp)

Elevation

Dark lifts by tone plus a 7–10 % ring; light lifts by a soft offset shadow. A real shadow is used only on things that float: the card, sheets and the toast.

Motion

Frequent things use standard springs with no visible bounce; rare things (the toast arriving, unlock, an import landing) get one soft overshoot. The open-in-place card is the one authored moment: the stub grows into the panel in 240 ms the first time, 180 ms after that, and rows settle 20 ms apart.

Iconography

Material Symbols Rounded, weight 400, optical size 24, grade −25 on dark. Filled only for selected or on. No emoji, no Unicode stand-ins.

Card rendering recipe

The slab is two gradients, a cached noise tile, two inset strokes, a groove and the type. Closed, the groove separates what opens the card (the bank row) from what copies (the number). Open, the stub below the number grows into expiry, CVV and name.

The same recipe in every state

Components

Every control maps to a Material 3 composable themed through the tokens. Each board is drawn twice, dark and light, with states forced side by side. Hover, press and keyboard focus also work live.

Feedback lab

Every toast and every copy state, live in one phone. There is no “Copied” toast and no timer on screen: the copied row confirms itself and returns, and the clipboard clears silently on the Settings value. The bottom-left belongs to Android’s own clipboard overlay for six seconds, so nothing of ours goes there.

Moments

The checkout card plus the twelve signature moments. Each one plays here with its reduced-motion version, its haptic, what TalkBack says, and how the first time differs from the hundredth. Budget: 240 ms the first time, 180 ms on repeats, input never blocked.

Usability & accessibility

Shown, not listed. Tap counts are walked step by step. The contrast table is computed from the tokens on load. Touch targets and the TalkBack order are measured from the frames on this page.

All views

Every screen and state at 390 × 844 dp, plus a 1024 dp tablet. Frames marked Live run the real behaviour. Copy is the R1 deck. New copy this direction adds is marked with a dotted underline when “New strings: Mark” is on in the toolbar.

Text budget

Visible words per screen: today, the R1 target, and this template, counted live from the frames on this page. A word is any token with a letter in it; digits, masks, icons and timer rings count as zero, as in R1.
