Obsidian, four ways

Round 2. Each variant keeps Obsidian and adds the same research-backed upgrades: about half the words, a custom toast instead of the stock snackbar, copy feedback on the field itself with the clipboard timer in the title bar, 12 signature moments you can play, a first card in about 7 taps, and Settings cut to 6 groups. They differ in how they make it feel premium.

Decisions these variants need from you

  1. No lock on an empty vault. First launch goes straight to “Add your first card”; the lock arms with the first saved card.
  2. Archive without a dialog. Archive happens at once with an Undo toast.
  3. “Pay faster” panel. Shown once after the second copy, offering autofill and the expiry and CVV notification.
  4. Settings sub-pages. App lock and card-list options move one level down to reach about 15 rows.
  5. Checkout only. Two tabs with Manage folded into Settings, and tags, notes and ⋮ only on an open card.
  6. Quiet only. Notes fold behind a small button on Home instead of showing two lines.
  7. New strings. “Set a screen lock”, “Notifications are off · Turn on”, “More details”, “Get the Excel sheet”, “Pay faster”. Each page can mark them.
