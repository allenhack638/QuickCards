Obsidian Quiet.
Only the number speaks.

Maximal restraint. Type and hairlines do the structure, tone does the depth, and the last four digits are the loudest thing on every screen. One accent, brand red, spent once per screen as a fill. No blur, no bounce, and exactly one shadow, under the toast. Short fades and slides, so it looks and feels the same on Android 8 as on Android 15.

Design system

Every value comes from one token object in this file. Swatches, the contrast table and the appendix JSON are generated from it. Depth is a tone step plus a hairline. The toast is the only thing that floats, so it carries the only shadow.

Colour

A neutral graphite ladder with no blue cast, six steps apart by tone alone. Hairlines at 7.5–8 % carry the structure; outlines on anything you operate clear 3:1. The only hue in the chrome is brand red, as a fill under white text, at most once per screen. The card is the only other colour.

Card bands

Unchanged from 01 Obsidian (OKLCH L 0.42). White text clears 8:1 on every band and the 76 % labels clear 5:1.

Network plates

Typography

Type does the work boxes do elsewhere. Geist for the chrome, with 400 as the default and 600 only for titles. Geist Mono only for values you read or copy. The card's last four is the largest type on Home after the screen title. Sizes follow Android 14's non-linear font scale.

Shape and spacing

Few radii, used consistently: 10 for chips and copy cells, 12 for controls, 14 for value groups and toasts, 16 for the card and grouped lists, 20–22 for dialogs and sheets. Spacing is a 4 dp grid with generous gaps between groups (22–28 dp) and tight ones inside (6–8 dp).

Spacing scale (dp)

Depth without shadow

Each level is one tone step and, where it overlaps content, a 1 dp ring at 13–14 %. In light, raised surfaces are white on a grey ground with the same ring. One exception: the toast is inverse (ink on light, a lifted graphite on dark) with a soft offset shadow, because it floats over moving content. Nothing depends on RenderEffect or blur, so API 26 and API 35 render the same.

Motion

Short fades and slides only: nothing scales, nothing bounces. The authored moment is the copy line, a 2 dp stroke that sweeps under the field you touched in 160 ms while the label slides from its name to “Copied”. Everything is under 240 ms the first time and under 180 ms after that. With Remove animations on, every motion becomes an instant state change and nothing is lost.

Iconography

Material Symbols Rounded, weight 400, optical size 24, grade −25 on dark. Outline by default, filled only for “selected” or “on”. In lists, icons appear only where they carry meaning (destructive rows, status); Settings rows have none.

The card

A compact slab: a 30 dp header line, a 48 dp number cell with no label, a hairline, and a 50 dp stub of Expiry, CVV and Name. About 132 dp, down from 225, so three cards and the start of a fourth fit above the navigation bar instead of one and a half. The ⋮ button spans the header and number rows so every target stays 48 dp. Four layers: band, a tone ramp, a 1 dp lit top edge, and the type. Tags, status, the due line and a note button share one quiet line under the slab.

Every state

Components

Each control maps to a Material 3 composable themed by the tokens. Every board is shown in dark and light, with states forced side by side. Hover, press and keyboard focus also work live.

Feedback lab

Every confirmation in the app, live. Copy never raises a toast and never shows a timer: the copied field confirms, and Android 13+ draws its own overlay bottom-left. The clipboard clears itself after the Settings value. QcToast is kept for undo, results and errors, one at a time, 8 dp above the navigation bar.

Moments

The twelve signature moments from the journey research, plus the owner’s top-bar search (SM13), each playable at full motion and with Remove animations. Budget: 240 ms or less the first time, 180 ms or less after that. Quiet adds no new kind of motion for any of them: it is the same fade and the same slide every time.

Usability & accessibility

Shown, not listed. The contrast table is computed from the tokens on load, touch targets are measured from a rendered frame, and the 200 % frames use the same components as everything else.
