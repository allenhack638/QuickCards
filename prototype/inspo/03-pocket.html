    Pocket. Your cards, in a well-made black wallet.

    QuickCards is a retrieval tool you open at a checkout with one hand. Pocket makes the cards themselves feel like the valuable objects they stand for: real materials, an embossed number, a back you can flip to for the CVV, all held in a stitched black pocket. Everything that is not a card stays matte, Material-shaped and calm, so the objects carry the screen and the controls never compete with them. The front card is always pulled out with its number, expiry, CVV and name ready to copy; every other card waits in the pocket as a 50 dp strip you can read and tap without scrolling.

    Only the cards are physical

    Material, emboss, light and stitch live on the cards and the pocket. Buttons, fields, sheets and lists are stock Material 3 shapes in matte tones.

    The values are the hero

    The number is the heaviest type on screen, set in IBM Plex Mono with a slashed zero, AAA on every band. Masked dots are dimmed; the last four are not.

    Every object has a flat twin

    TalkBack, 200 % text and tablets get the open list: each card is one node with every copy and reveal as a custom action.

    What changes versus the current QuickCards

    • wallet
      Home becomes a pocket. One card is pulled out in full; the rest show their top strip (bank, last four, holder, status, network) inside a stitched pocket. Six cards fit on one screen instead of two and a half.
    • texture
      Bands become materials. Fifteen bands map to three finishes (anodised, lacquer, soft-touch), each retuned so white text is at least 7:1 at the band's base.
    • flip
      CVV lives on the back. Holding CVV flips the card to its signature strip for 5 s. With Remove animations on it crossfades instead.
    • format_size
      Five type roles with real jumps (30 / 24 / 18 / 16 / 14) in Manrope, plus Plex Mono for values only. Titles grow from 24 to 30.
    • accessibility_new
      One node per card for TalkBack, headings everywhere, real selection semantics, initial focus in dialogs, disabled buttons that say why, and a Delete dialog that matches its Undo.

    Design system

    Matte black and steel for everything you operate, real materials for the things you carry. Tokens below are generated from one object, which also feeds the contrast table and the JSON in the appendix.

    Components

    Every control maps to a Material 3 composable. States are shown forced (hover, pressed, focus-visible, disabled, error) in both themes side by side; the real ones also respond to your pointer and keyboard.

    Usability and accessibility

    The pocket is the sighted, one-handed fast path. Everything it does has a flat, linear twin that TalkBack, Switch Access, 200 % text and keyboards use without asking: the open list. Nothing here relies on colour, motion or a gesture alone.

    Views

    Every screen from the inventory at 390×844 (1 px = 1 dp), grouped by flow. Phones marked touch_applive respond: tap a value to copy, hold number or CVV for 450 ms, tap a strip to pull its card out, tap More.
