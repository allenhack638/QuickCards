Expressive

QuickCards as a first-class Pixel app. Confident Roboto Flex type, shapes that answer your thumb, and calm tonal surfaces that let each card's own colour do the talking. The job is retrieval, so the expression goes exactly where the value is: the last four is the heaviest thing on screen, every value sits in its own full-height cell, and a copied value blooms into a small red cookie with a check. Everything else is stock Material 3 Expressive, themed, so a Compose developer builds it from the catalogue rather than from custom drawing.

touch_appOpen → fingerprint → 1 tap to a copied number, expiry, CVV or name, for every card on the list.

The card is the colour

Surfaces come from a low-chroma mauve-grey seed in five tonal steps per theme. The only hues on screen are each card's band and one red commit: Unlock, Save, or the copied cookie.

Shape is feedback

Buttons squish from pill to 8 or 12dp while pressed, selected things round off, a copied value morphs into a cookie. No state relies on colour alone: each change also has a shape, an icon or a word.

One tap to every value

The pass is a connected group: a full-band face with the number, then three stub cells for Expiry, CVV and Name. Each is a full-height copy target, 64dp tall, and each reacts to its neighbours.

What changes versus the current QuickCards

  • confirmation_numberThe Pass Stub becomes an M3 Expressive connected group. The perforation is the 2dp group gap, and the 12dp inner corners pinch into the stub notch. The band fills the whole face instead of a 48dp strip.
  • format_sizeThe last four is set in Roboto Flex at width 80, 30sp, the heaviest type on screen. Masked groups drop to small dimmed mono dots, so the four digits you identify a card by are what you see.
  • content_copyCopy confirms on the exact cell with a spring cookie-shape morph in brand red, a Confirm haptic, a polite live-region announcement and the existing snackbar. Hold-to-reveal animates the font's width axis as its own progress indicator.
  • addAdd moves from the app bar to an M3E FAB menu holding the three ways in that already exist (Add card, Tap your card, Import). Theme becomes an inline connected button group that falls back to the radio dialog at large text.
  • view_agendaManage, Settings, sheets and dialog lists become grouped segments with 40dp tonal icon containers. Destructive confirms get an error-tonal button, and the Delete dialog drops "This can't be undone", because the snackbar offers Undo.

Colour

A neutral tonal palette generated from a low-chroma seed (hue 330°, chroma about 4) and frozen in Color.kt: no dynamic colour, so the brand red stays single and stable. Dark and light are equal: each step is tuned on its own, not inverted. The ladder steps are wider than today's (the audit found 2–5% steps), and every interactive boundary clears 3:1.

Surface ladder and roles

Text, outline, state and feedback

Card bands

Sixteen bands retuned to equal perceptual lightness (OKLCH L ≈ 0.36–0.42), so white text clears 7:1 on every solid band and 4.5:1 at the gradient's lightest corner, where the bank name sits. Ratios are computed live from the values below. Custom colours pass the same guard.

Network plates

Simple typographic and geometric plates on a white 40dp circle (Google Wallet's logo disc, adapted). They are labelled for TalkBack, and custom networks keep a text badge.

Typography

Roboto Flex carries the whole UI. Its wdth axis condenses the hero digits (80) and screen titles (96), and its GRAD axis drops to −25 in dark so light-on-dark text doesn't bloom, with no change in width. Roboto Mono is used only where characters must line up and be read one by one: stub values, mask dots, counts and the number field. Every number uses tnum. Both families ship in res/font (no downloadable fonts), and both are free on Google Fonts.

Shape, spacing, elevation

The M3 Expressive shape scale, used literally. 28dp (ExtraLarge) is reserved for the pass, sheets and dialogs, so the card silhouette stays the roundest object on screen. Grouped lists step down to 20dp (LargeIncreased) outer corners with 4dp inner corners, and fields use 12dp top corners.

Spacing

A 4dp base on an 8dp rhythm. Gutters are 16dp, grouped gaps 2dp, the gap between passes 12dp, and section gaps 24dp. Space above a heading is always larger than space below it.

Elevation

Tone first, then a ring, then a soft offset shadow. On dark surfaces a shadow alone is invisible, so every raised surface also gets a 1dp white ring at 6–10%. Light theme uses warm, offset two-layer shadows. The pass face casts a shadow tinted from its own band in light.

Motion

Material physics springs, converted to CSS linear() easings at load from their damping and stiffness, so the page moves exactly like Compose's MotionScheme. Chrome uses the Standard scheme (no bounce). The Expressive FastSpatial spring (0.6 / 800, visible overshoot) is spent on one moment only: the copy confirmation. Remove animations snaps every spring and keeps every state change.

The authored moment: copy morph and width-axis reveal

Tap any value on the pass. The cell squishes and widens on press (button-group behaviour: its neighbours give way), then the label swaps to Copied, and a 20dp circle springs into a 9-lobe Cookie filled with brand red, carrying a check. After 900ms it springs back into the label. A Confirm haptic fires, the polite live region says Card number copied. Clears in 1 minute., and the snackbar appears.

Press and hold the number or CVV for 450ms. While you hold, the last four widens along Roboto Flex's wdth axis from 80 to 100. The font itself is the hold progress. When the reveal opens, all digits show and condense back from 100 to 80 over the 5-second window, beside a 3dp drain line with an M3E stop dot. Then it re-masks.

Reduced motion (toggle at left, or Android's Remove animations): there is no spring and no width animation. The cookie appears in its final shape with a 150ms crossfade, the reveal still needs the 450ms hold, and the drain is replaced by a plain countdown, Hidden in 5 s.

Keyboard: Enter copies the focused value; R reveals it (the custom action Show card number in TalkBack).

Iconography

Material Symbols Rounded, one setting: weight 400, grade 0 (−25 in dark), optical size 24 (20 for 20dp icons). Fill is 0 at rest and 1 only for selected navigation and the pinned mark. Icons sit in 40dp tonal containers in Settings and Manage, which turns a column of identical grey glyphs into scannable landmarks.

Card rendering recipe

Five layers, all Brush and drawBehind calls that run on API 26. There are no bitmaps, no blur and no shader. The face is never flat, and the sheen stays at or below 8% so white text keeps contrast.

Six bands, then every special state

Buttons, button groups, FAB menu

Every button is a pill at rest and morphs to its pressed corner while held: 8dp for 40dp buttons, 12dp for 56dp buttons (M3E PressedContainerShape, Standard FastSpatial spring). Press and hold any button below to feel it. Filled brand red appears once per screen. Destructive confirms use error-tonal, never brand red.

Chips

Unselected chips have an outline at 3:1 or better. Selected chips change in three ways: a tonal fill, a check (the avatar morphs into it), and a shape that rounds from 8 to 16dp. "All" gets the check too, which fixes the audit's fill-only selection.

Text fields and search

M3 filled fields with Expressive 12dp top corners. The label is always visible: it sits inside the empty field, never a placeholder, and floats when there is a value. Focus thickens the indicator to 3dp in on-surface, never red, and keyboard focus adds the 2dp ring. An error has three signals: the error indicator, a trailing error icon, and text that names the fix.

Checkbox, radio, switch

Selection controls stay neutral: on-surface fill, never red. The switch thumb grows on press and carries a check when on, so it doesn't rely on colour alone. Every control sits in a 48dp target.

The pass, in every state

Every value cell on these passes is live. Tap one to copy it, or hold the number or the CVV to reveal it.

Copied-card notification

This is the system template, not a custom layout. It is silent, has no buttons, and is private on the lock screen (public version: Card details hidden). The small icon is the Stub Lock mark.

Usability & accessibility

The target is "wow", not just compliant. Numbers are AAA, a TalkBack user handles a whole card from one focus stop, the pass reflows at 200% instead of truncating, every action has a button equivalent, and nothing relies on colour alone. Everything below is computed or live, not asserted.

Contrast, computed from the tokens on this page

WCAG 2.2 relative-luminance ratios, calculated in the browser from the CSS variables (including the color-mix() tints). Targets: 7:1 for card numbers, values and primary labels; 4.5:1 for all other text; 3:1 for non-text boundaries.

200% font scale: reflow, never truncate

These frames render Android 14's non-linear 200% curve: 12sp becomes 24, 16 becomes 30, and the 30sp number becomes 48. The number wraps only between digit groups, and the stub reflows from one row of three to Expiry and CVV side by side over a full-width Name, so no value ever splits. The bank name and card name wrap instead of ellipsising, and dialogs move below the app bar and scroll with their actions pinned. Theme drops the inline button group and goes back to its radio dialog.

Touch targets and the thumb zone

The dashed boxes are the real hit areas, measured from the layout. Every target is at least 48×48dp. The stub cells are only 2dp apart visually, but each is 66dp tall and at least 93dp wide, so a slightly-off tap still lands on the value it is nearest. Copy targets are whole cells, not words. The primary actions (FAB, Save, Unlock, Import) sit in the bottom third, where one-handed thumbs reach. The FAB slides away while you scroll down, so it never sits over a value you are reaching for.

TalkBack: one stop per card, every action in the menu

Each pass is one merged focus node with a spoken summary. Copy and reveal of each value, plus Pin, Edit, Archive and Delete, are custom actions in TalkBack's Actions menu. That takes a ten-card list from about 100 swipes to 10, the 1Password rotor pattern. Spoken strings use the existing cards_a11y_* resources. Strings marked proposed are new resources this template asks for.

Focus, keyboard and motion

Press Tab anywhere on this page. Every control shows a 2dp on-surface ring with a 2dp offset: about 14:1 against the ground, and white on card faces. The ring sits outside the control, so it never covers content. On a phone, focus moves into a sheet or dialog on open (to its pane title), and returns to the trigger on close. Try it below: open the ⋮ sheet with the keyboard, press Esc, and focus lands back on ⋮.
