23 Oct 2026 Starting point

The existing app

The Android app before the redesign (“v2 Polished”, 19 screens). A design audit against Material 3 and the project’s own design rules scored it 7 out of 16.

What was wrong

  • Copying a number took three taps: open the card, find the tile, confirm.
  • Search and filter sat on a second header row, wasting space on five screens.
  • The floating add button covered card content; sticky footers clipped form fields.
  • Nested screens were inconsistent about the bottom bar; the tablet grid showed 2 of 3 cards.
  • Test content shipped in the UI, and three type families competed.

23 Oct 2026 Direction

Choosing a direction

Three decisions came first: copy straight from the home list, one fingerprint opens a 60-second unlock window, and CVV stays masked but copies with a tap. Four visual directions were then sketched — Form Ink, Pass Stub, a High-Density Grid and a category-standard layout — and Pass Stub was chosen: every card is a boarding pass, built to be read at a counter.

23 Oct 2026 v2.1

App lock and one icon system

QuickCards now opens to a lock screen and locks itself after a period without use, and every icon comes from Material Symbols Rounded, the Android system icon family.

App lock

  • Lock screen on launch, after inactivity, when you leave the app, or from Lock now.
  • One fingerprint unlocks the app and starts the instant copy window.
  • Open sheets, dialogs, menus, revealed values and the notification close when it locks.
  • Settings: App lock on or off; Lock after inactivity from 10 seconds to Never; Lock when you leave the app.

Icons

  • Material Symbols Rounded everywhere in v2, at 24dp, weight 400.
  • Selected tab icons fill in; field confirmations use a heavier check.
  • Fingerprint, lock, card and shield glyphs match Android’s own BiometricPrompt and settings.
  • v1 keeps its original icons as a record of that iteration.

23 Oct 2026 v2.2

Backups carry settings and update saved cards

Export now includes your settings, and import shows a review of the backup before anything changes. Cards you’ve already saved can be updated with the backup’s details (the default) or kept as they are, and settings can be restored or left alone. The full flow is documented under Backup and restore.

Export

  • Cards with every field, all five managed lists, and settings (on by default).
  • Passphrase of at least 8 characters, entered twice.
  • File records the app, format version and export date.

Import

  • Open with the passphrase, then review counts: new, changed and same cards; new list items; settings.
  • Choose: update saved cards, or keep what’s on this phone.
  • Choose: restore settings, or not.
  • Result: “1 added · 1 updated · 6 unchanged · settings restored”.

24 Oct 2026 Build walkthrough

One name, one network rule, no countdown

A walkthrough of the build settled four things: the card form asks for less, the pass says who holds the card, the network is never a question, and locking is invisible until it happens.

Card form and pass

  • No Card network field. The network always follows the number’s first digits by the longest match. It appears only as the badge in the number field and on the pass; a number that matches nothing shows no badge and is checked with the generic rule, 12 to 19 digits and Luhn. Every save stores the card as auto-detected.
  • Card holder is the only name. “Name on card” and “Card name” are gone, with their validation. The stub NAME is the holder’s name in capitals, the band shows the bank alone, and titles, search and the notification use the bank plus the card type when set.
  • Discard confirmation. Leaving Add or Edit with changes asks “Discard this card?” with Keep editing and an error-coloured Discard; nothing changed leaves immediately.
  • Flush supporting lines. Every helper, error and character counter sits on the field’s outline edges, in the form and in dialogs.

Locking and settings

  • No lock chip, no countdown. The Cards chip row starts with the holder chips and Lock now lives in Settings, whose supporting text is “Lock QuickCards right away”.
  • Idle time only. The unlock window and the inactivity lock both restart on any touch, key, scroll or typing. “Lock after inactivity” reads “After 1 minute without use”; “Instant copy window” reads “Copy without a fingerprint until 1 minute of no use”.
  • Settings copy and icons. “Show expiry in notification” explains “Month and year in the notification”; “Lock when you leave the app” explains “When you switch apps or lock the phone”; “Tags per card” gets its own list icon.

Fixes carried in

  • Locking covers everything. The inactivity lock closes every sheet, dialog, menu and the keyboard, and nothing draws above the lock screen.
  • One name and one network. The removed fields and the manual network override are gone from the form; a card saved before this change keeps its stored values, but nothing reads them.
  • Focus and save. A focused field and its helper line stay above the Save bar; a blocked save always shows an error on the right field, and a picked bank never changes itself.
  • Layout. The pass shares the 16dp gutter, the search bar matches it, the 2-column grid and 360dp phones were checked, and the Add/Edit preview is compact on short screens.
  • Consistency. One dialog surface, larger gaps before trailing switches, no single-word orphans, a distinct Tags per card icon, and “Month and year in the notification” for expiry.
  • Security and startup. Turning off app lock, Never for inactivity, lock-on-leave off and showing the CVV in the notification ask for confirmation; startup work moved off the main thread and Manage counts wait for the first database emission.

24 Oct 2026 Demo round 2

Pins, one picker, and a red Save that waits for a change

The second walkthrough tightened the everyday paths: settings look like settings, the card form asks for less typing and offers the same picker everywhere, and pinned cards stay on top without turning the pass into a different component.

Pinned cards

  • More sheet. “Copy number, expiry and name” became Copy details and copies the same three values. “Move to top” became Pin card / Unpin card with a filled push_pin.
  • Pinned first. Pinned cards come before the rest in pin order (first pinned on top) in the list, the grid, holder filters and search. A 16dp pin glyph sits in the band before the network label.
  • Limit. Cards › Pinned cards offers 1, 3, 5 or 10, default 3, with “Up to 3 cards at the top”. At the limit, Pin is disabled in the card menu with “3 of 3 pinned. Unpin a card or raise the limit in Settings.” (no dialog, no automatic unpinning).
  • Backups. Format 2 writes each card’s pin state and the limit; older backups restore their cards unpinned.

Settings and inputs

  • Theme is a row. Appearance shows a normal two-line Theme row whose supporting text is System default, Light or Dark; tapping it opens the shared radio dialog with those three options. The segmented control is gone.
  • One picker. Card holder, Bank and Card type open the same bottom sheet, “Choose card holder”, “Choose bank” and “Choose card type”, with the item’s lead, a check on the current choice, search above seven items and a final Add … row. Card type starts with None.
  • Limits while typing. Number, expiry, CVV, note, tag and every Manage name keep only the filtered value at the field’s limit, with the caret at the end; pasting is trimmed the same way.
  • Red actions, dirty Save. Filled actions stay brand red with system colours on; dynamic colour tints surfaces only. In Edit, Save changes stays disabled until something changes, as does Save in every Manage edit dialog.
  • Tag row. The Add button is 56dp tall and centred on the field box, with the 0/16 counter flush under the field.

24 Oct 2026 Scan card

Read a card with the camera, entirely on the phone

Add and Edit can now fill the number and expiry from the camera. The OCR model is bundled in the APK, frames are never stored or logged, the CVV is never read, and the app stays offline.

In the form

  • Camera action. Card number carries a 48dp camera icon; a detected network badge sits immediately to its left, with the 16dp end inset kept.
  • Permission. The first tap opens the system camera prompt. A denial shows “Camera access needed” with Not now and Allow; a permanent denial turns the second action into Open settings. No camera hardware hides the icon.
  • Fill. A scan goes through the normal field rules: number grouped and detected, expiry normalised, CVV left empty and focused. Snackbar: “Card number and expiry filled. Check them before saving.”

On the phone

  • Bundled model. ML Kit bundled Latin text recognition (no Play-services download) with CameraX 1.3.4. No network permission and no transport scheduler in the merged manifest.
  • Scan screen. Live preview under a dark scrim, ISO ID-1 frame (85.6:54, 18dp corners, 2dp white stroke), “Scan card”, “Hold the card inside the frame”, Close and a torch toggle when the camera has a flash.
  • Reading. 720p ImageAnalysis (keep only latest), cropped to the frame; the same number must appear in two consecutive analyses, an expiry can come from the last three. Nothing in 20 seconds → “Couldn't read the card. Try more light or type the number.” with Try again and Type instead.
  • Privacy. No frame is stored or logged; the camera and recogniser close on dispose; scanning counts as activity; the app lock closes the camera.

Superseded by it11: the in-app scanner was removed to keep the app small and camera-free; its parser now cleans pasted or keyboard-scanned text.

24 Oct 2026 Keyboard scan

Keyboard scan & smart paste

The in-app camera scanner was removed to keep the app small and camera-free. Card details can still be scanned with the keyboard’s own scan tool where the keyboard offers it; pasted or scanned text is cleaned and fills number and expiry (never CVV). No camera permission.

  • Small and camera-free. CameraX and the bundled ML Kit OCR model are gone; the debug APK drops from 61.2 MiB to 21.5 MiB and no camera permission is declared or merged.
  • Smart paste. One input event that inserts two or more characters is parsed: the number is grouped and detected through the normal rules, and an expiry fills an empty Expiry field. Typing one character at a time is unchanged.
  • Copy. “Card number filled from the pasted text” or “Card number and expiry filled from the pasted text”. Junk text keeps the field’s existing digit filter, with no snackbar.
  • Never the CVV. Pasted or scanned text never fills the CVV; it keeps its own filter. The card-number field carries only the network badge, 16dp from the outline’s end edge.
  • Parser. The old scan parser is now CardTextParser in :domain; its 25 fixtures are unchanged.

24 Oct 2026 Card list care

Expired, archive, notes & legacy repair

Real vaults predate this version of the app, and cards that are expired or finished still deserve to be kept without cluttering the list. This step repairs legacy formats once, flags what it cannot fix, greys an expired pass, labels one that expires soon, adds an archive with a reason, and puts the note on the card itself. Nothing new leaves the phone and every value stays one tap from the clipboard.

Before

  • The in-place upgrade and every import copied expiry, number and CVV exactly as stored. Raw values such as 11//22 or 13/22 reached the pass and the form with no warning.
  • An expired card looked identical to a live one and stayed in the list until it was deleted.
  • A card that was no longer used could only be deleted; its history and reason were lost.
  • Notes were encrypted and searchable but only appeared in the card’s ⋮ sheet header.

After

  • One repair. A one-time, non-destructive normaliser fixes number, expiry and CVV on upgrade and on import. Before values are kept for 30 days and Edit shows “Changed from …” with Undo. A single banner reports the result; unfixable cards carry Check details and a Needs check filter.
  • Expired pass. After its expiry month a card renders in neutral graphite — band, sheen, body and edge — with an outlined Expired chip on the number label row. Values stay full contrast; copy, hold-to-reveal, edit, pin, archive and delete all keep working.
  • Expires soon is a plain label on the same row (no chip, no grey, no behaviour change) within the chosen 1, 2 or 3 month window.
  • Archive. The ⋮ sheet gains Archive above Delete with an optional 40-character reason. Archived cards live in Manage › Archived cards, searchable by digits, bank, holder, network, type, tags, note or reason, and come back with Restore. Archiving unpins.
  • Notes on the pass. Up to two lines under the tags, with More / Less only when it overflows; a 500 ms hold copies the note.
  • One settings section. Settings › Card list: Expired cards (keep, move to the end, hide), Expires soon (1–3 months), Archived cards (Archived only, or also in search) and Show notes on cards.

Copy, verbatim

Expired · Expires soon · Archive / Archived / Restore · Check details · Note. The archive dialog says “It moves to Manage › Archived cards. You can copy, edit or restore it there.” Snackbars are “Card archived” + Undo, “Card restored” and “Note copied”. Never “inactive”, “old”, “deleted” or “hidden card”.

Breathing room, status row & colours

The Add/Edit form gets more space around its fields, the pass status flags move onto one compact row, and the palette grows from five bands to sixteen plus a custom colour. The card's colour still lives in the same TEXT column, so nothing migrates and nothing leaves the phone.

  • Status on the bottom row. Expired, Check details, Expires soon and Archived move from the crowded CARD NUMBER row to the right end of the tags row; the number row keeps only the type pill. Cards without tags still get the row when they have a status.
  • Slightly more room. Passes sit 16dp apart; the number, details and bottom rows each gain a few dp; every Add/Edit field has the same 36dp gap to the next; Undo for a repaired value sits on its "Changed from" line.
  • Small fixes. Archived cards use the full list width; Manage › Archived cards shows its count like the other rows; old-app files import without a passphrase field; the repair banner reads "Fixed the format of {n} cards."
  • Sixteen bands, one grid. Navy, Rust, Forest, Plum and Graphite keep their keys and hexes; Teal, Ocean, Indigo, Violet, Magenta, Wine, Crimson, Burnt orange, Olive, Moss and Slate join them. Swatches are 40dp discs in 48dp targets, eight per row at 412dp and six at 360dp, with the neutral selected ring and a white check as before. Every band clears 7:1 against white.
  • Custom colour. A seventeenth swatch with a conic rainbow outline opens Custom colour: an HSV wheel and brightness slider, a six-character uppercase Hex field with the reserved helper line, a live mini pass preview (“Preview” band over its body tint) and up to five Recent swatches. A colour under 4.5:1 against white shows “Too light for white card text. Choose a darker shade.” and disables Use colour. Actions are Cancel / Use colour.

Design system v2

Pass Stub on Material 3. Everything below is rendered from the same stylesheet the v2 prototype uses, so it cannot drift from the build. Switch the page theme in the top bar.

Principles

Copy is the primary action

Every value on a card is a 48dp-plus copy target on the home list. No screen has to be opened to copy.

Private by default

Numbers show only their last 4 digits and CVVs are dots. Tap copies the real value; hold reveals it for 5 seconds.

One fingerprint, one window

The first sensitive action opens an unlock window. Inside it, everything is instant; any touch, key, scroll or typing keeps it alive, and nothing on screen counts it down.

At most one red per screen

Brand red marks the single primary or destructive action. Selection, navigation and chips stay neutral.

Colour belongs to the pass

Only the pass carries a hue: its band and a faint tint of its body. The chrome stays graphite or paper.

Nothing changes silently

Renaming anything that cards use lists those cards and asks first. Anything in use cannot be deleted.

Backup and restore

How a backup is made, what it contains, and exactly what happens to each piece when it comes back. Both directions need the unlock window, and nothing leaves the phone except the file you save.

Export, step by step

  1. Settings, Backup, Export backup. If the copy window has ended, the fingerprint prompt opens first: “Confirm it’s you to export your cards”.
  2. Include settings is ticked by default; untick it for a cards-only backup.
  3. Enter a passphrase (at least 8 characters) and confirm it. Export stays disabled until both match; a mismatch shows “Passphrases don’t match”.
  4. The file is saved to Downloads as quickcards-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.qcb, and a snackbar confirms: “Backup with settings saved to Downloads”.

The whole file is encrypted with the passphrase: AES-256-GCM with a key derived by PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 over 600,000 iterations. The passphrase cannot be recovered; without it the file is unreadable.

Import, step by step

  1. Settings, Backup, Import backup, after the fingerprint prompt if needed.
  2. Choose backup file, enter its passphrase, tap Open. A wrong passphrase shows “That passphrase doesn’t open this backup” and nothing changes.
  3. Review backup shows the file and export date, then: cards as new · changed · same, Formats fixed: {n} · Need a check: {m}, and for each list how many items are new. Settings show as “included” when present.
  4. If any saved cards differ, choose Update with the backup’s details (default) or Keep what’s on this phone.
  5. If the backup has settings, Restore settings from the backup is ticked by default.
  6. Import applies everything at once and reports the result, for example “1 added · 1 updated · 6 unchanged · settings restored”. Cancel at any point changes nothing.

Edge cases

  • Card numbers are unique. A backup can never create a second card with a number you already have; it can only update or keep it.
  • Tag limits are not applied to imported cards; like cards that predate a lower limit, they keep their tags and cannot gain more until under the limit.
  • Same-name holders merge. Two different people with the same name become one holder; rename one before exporting to keep them apart.
  • Restored settings take effect at once, including theme and lock times. The app does not lock immediately on restore.
  • Older backups (format 1, from the current Android app) must stay importable: cards and banks only, no settings. The review shows “Settings: not in this backup”. Every import runs the legacy normaliser before the review, so raw values such as 11//22 are fixed and counted, and anything unfixable is flagged instead of failing the import.
  • Erase all data followed by import restores a phone completely from one file.

Rules and validation

Every input checks itself when you leave it and again on save. An error turns the outline and label red, replaces the helper text with what is wrong and how to fix it, and moves focus to the first problem. Helper lines are reserved, so errors never shift the layout. Text is trimmed and repeated spaces collapsed before any check.
