Try

  • Tap the masked number, expiry, CVV or name to copy it. Hold the number or CVV to see it for 5 seconds.
  • After copying a number, a notification shows expiry and CVV for 30 seconds.
  • Use the holder chips to switch between card holders.
  • Pin a card from its More sheet to keep it at the top of the list.
  • Manage holders, banks, networks, types and tags from the Manage tab. Try renaming or deleting one that cards use.
  • Archive a card from its ⋮ sheet and find it again in Manage › Archived cards; restore it from the same menu.
  • Long-press a note on a card to copy it, or use More to read the whole note. Notes, expired cards and archived search are set in Settings › Card list.
  • Tune masking, notifications, tags and more in Settings.
  • The app opens locked. Leave it untouched for the time set in Settings and it locks again.
  • Swipe in from the left edge or press Esc to go back.
